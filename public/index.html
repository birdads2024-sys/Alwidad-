Al Widad - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

100% Free Educational Application

1. Overview & Educational Purpose

Al Widad is a completely free, non-commercial educational mobile application designed exclusively to support high school students in learning mathematics. Our mission is to provide open, accessible, high-quality learning resources, video lessons, and interactive quizzes at no financial cost to students.

2. No Purchases or Subscriptions

Al Widad does not offer any paid content, digital subscriptions, in-app purchases, or commercial services. All educational materials within the application are 100% free and accessible to all registered students without any hidden fees or external payment requirements.

3. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized learning experience, we collect minimal user account information during registration:

  • Account Details: Name and email address used for secure account authentication.
  • Academic Category: Selected grade or academic section to display relevant math curriculum.
  • Learning Progress: Saved quiz results and watched lesson history to help students track their study progress.

4. Data Protection & Privacy

We deeply respect user privacy and adhere to strict data security standards:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user personal data to any third-party advertisers or commercial entities.
  • We do not track users across third-party websites or applications.
  • User data is securely encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard cloud security practices.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

Students have full control over their personal information. You have the right to inspect, update, or request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team.

6. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provided by Google for safe authentication and data storage. These services process data in compliance with strict global privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Support & Privacy Concerns:

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at support@alwidad-app.com.
